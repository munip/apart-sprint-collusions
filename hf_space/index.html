Collusion-Watch

This page presents a benchmark and monitor for distinguishing benign cooperation from harmful coordination in simulated multi-agent markets.

The source archive has 112 complete episodes: 30 A2, 41 A3, and 41 A5; A2 seed 20 is absent. On the 16-episode seed-disjoint test set, M2 AUROC is 0.15 (base) vs 0.58 (fine-tuned), but neither flags a collusive episode. M4 flags all 16 episodes.
