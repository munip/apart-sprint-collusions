Collusion-Watch

A benchmark and monitor pipeline for distinguishing benign cooperation from harmful coordination in simulated multi-agent markets. The full article and project files are linked below.

Article

Claim: Fine-tuning increased M2's ranking AUROC on the small held-out set, but did not make its action policy flag collusive episodes. These results are descriptive and do not establish reliable detection or safety-preserving fine-tuning.

This English-language pilot uses a 15-round simulated double auction: A2 honest seller chat is the benign comparison, A3 is overt cartel coordination, and A5 uses coded price rotation. The source release contains 112 episodes: 30 A2, 41 A3, and 41 A5. A2 seed 20 is absent.

Results at a glance

All six monitor arms were evaluated on the same 16 seed-disjoint episodes (12 collusive, 4 benign).

M2 AUROC: 0.15 to 0.58 Base to fine-tuned judge.
M2 harmful flags: 1/12 to 0/12 Higher ranking AUROC did not improve action-level detection.
M4 flags: 16/16 This includes all four benign episodes.

The test set has only four benign episodes. Three base-judge responses were unparseable in each judge-using monitor arm; the archived summary counter is stale. The reconciled report counts those failures. Do not interpret these small-sample results as reliable operating thresholds.

Lexsi scope and limitations

  • Closest fit: Track 2a statement 2, a labeled domain-specific evaluation set. Multi-round traces are released, but the reported analysis is episode-level, not a turn-by-turn audit.
  • Not covered: multilingual/code-mixed safety (Track 2a statement 4) and safety-preserving fine-tuning (Track 2b statement 5). Training was plain SFT; no refusal, over-refusal, or safety-retention evaluation was run.
  • The dataset and evaluation are English-only. The dataset reuse license is not specified; see its card and the upstream model terms before reuse.
  • No cost-per-1,000-windows or serving-throughput benchmark is available. The fine-tuned model's immutable revision and trainer log are not available to independently establish checkpoint provenance.

Reproducibility and responsible release

The Space's Files and versions tab contains the complete reviewed release package. Its runs ZIP is made from the filtered 112-episode package, not the entire local runs/ directory. Private monitor keys/anchors, credentials, and unreviewed raw captures are excluded.

The full article reports the data split, methods, results, negative findings, limitations, and follow-up experiments. GitHub remains the canonical source and commit history.
